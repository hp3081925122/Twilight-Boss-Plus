# Twilight Boss Plus

**An add-on for The Twilight Forest that reworks all eight bosses** — new skill sets, health-based phases and self-drawn pixel-art effects instead of piles of vanilla particles. Every boss has its own style, and no two bosses share the same skill or effect.

## Features

- **Phases** – bosses change behaviour at health percentages and force signature skills when entering a new phase.
- **Fair telegraphs** – every dangerous skill is warned on the ground or in the air before it hits.
- **Scaling damage** – skill damage = boss attack × skill multiplier; knockback is low and respects knockback resistance.
- **Pixel-art VFX** – custom textured meshes, ribbons and decals rendered in a pixel style.
- **Fully configurable** – one config file per boss (`config/twilightbossplus/`): toggle the rework, health, damage, cooldowns, phases and every skill. Client config for effect quality and screen shake.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47+
- The Twilight Forest 4.3+
